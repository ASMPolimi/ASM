Terms & Conditions

Rules, guidelines, and terms governing membership, community initiatives, and usage of asmpolimi.it.

Last Updated: October 2026

Welcome to ASM POLIMI: By using our website (asmpolimi.it), applying for membership, or registering for our on-campus activities, you agree to these Terms & Conditions and our association bylaws.

1. About the Association

Associazione Studenti Musulmani - Politecnico di Milano (ASM POLIMI) is an officially recognized, non-profit university student association active at Politecnico di Milano (Piazza Leonardo da Vinci 32, 20133 Milano, Italy).

Our mission is to foster community, support academic success, advocate for student prayer and reflection spaces, offer halal lifestyle navigation tools, and promote interfaith understanding and cultural dialogue across all university campuses.

2. Membership Application & Membership Fee

  • Eligibility: Membership is open to all students, researchers, alumni, and staff of Politecnico di Milano, as well as students of neighboring universities who support our community values.
  • Application Process: Prospective members apply voluntarily via our online form (asmpolimi.it/register). Applications are verified by the board.
  • Annual Fee: Where applicable, an annual associative contribution (e.g. €5.00 or as determined by the annual assembly) directly funds student welcome kits (including custom 3D keychains), academic workshops, event subsidies, and associative materials.
  • No Commercial Intent: Contributions are strictly non-profit associative contributions, not commercial purchase transactions.

3. Event Participation & Attendance Policy

  • Seat Reservation: Because campus event spaces (e.g. aula magna, student hubs) have statutory capacity limits, event registrations are allocated on a first-come, first-served basis or member-priority booking.
  • Cancellation Courtesy: If you register for an event but can no longer attend, please notify the organizers at least 24 hours in advance so your seat can be released to students on the waiting list.
  • On-Campus Behavior: All attendees must abide by Politecnico di Milano university regulations, respect fellow participants, and uphold a peaceful, welcoming atmosphere.

4. Disclaimer for Digital Services & Tools

Our website provides free, volunteer-maintained student tools:

  • Daily Prayer Timings: Calculated using recognized astronomical algorithms for Milan metropolitan coordinates (45.46° N, 9.19° E). While we strive for absolute accuracy, slight timing variations of 1–2 minutes may occur based on geographical terrain.
  • Halal Scanner & Product Search: This tool utilizes the Open Food Facts API and community-flagged submissions to help students identify potentially non-halal additives (e.g. E120, animal gelatin). Product formulations change frequently by manufacturers; the tool is an informational aid, and students should always check the physical ingredient label on packaging.
  • "Ask an Imam" Feature: Responses provided through our platform are religious and pastoral educational guidance from qualified community scholars. They do not constitute legal, medical, or formal university disciplinary advice.

Users agree not to:

  • Submit false, abusive, or malicious information through membership or event forms.
  • Attempt to bypass rate limits, probe private administrative endpoints, or disrupt server infrastructure.
  • Use automated scripts or scrapers against our backend APIs in a manner that degrades performance for other students.
