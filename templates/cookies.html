1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently, remember your preferences, and provide aggregated analytics to site owners.

Local Storage: In addition to standard cookies, modern web applications utilize HTML5 LocalStorage to save settings (such as your chosen site language) directly on your device without transmitting unnecessary data with every network request.

B. Performance & Analytics (Google tag / Google Analytics 4)

Our website utilizes the official Google tag (gtag.js) with measurement ID G-8N5B01YCZ4 (Google Analytics 4).

Purpose of Google Analytics: As a volunteer student organization, we use aggregated, anonymous telemetry to understand:

  • Which pages are most helpful to students (e.g., daily prayer times, Friday Jumu'ah room announcements, Halal scanner).
  • Device screen resolutions and browser types, helping us optimize mobile layouts for student smartphones.
  • General geographic city-level location (e.g. Milan vs. international exchange applicants).

Google Analytics 4 operates with IP Anonymization enabled by default and does not store full IP addresses. We do not use Google Signals or cross-device commercial advertising remarketing.

3. Third-Party Links & External Services

Our website links to legitimate external services that may have their own independent cookie policies when you leave our domain:

  • Google Drive: Used for voluntary access to community photo gallery archives.
  • Politecnico di Milano Official Portals: Links to official university maps, campus directories, and classroom locators.
  • Open Food Facts: Public barcode database queries for halal product ingredients verification.

4. How to Control, Block, or Delete Cookies

You can manage, restrict, or delete cookies at any time via your browser settings. Please note that disabling technical storage may affect language preferences.

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Third-party cookies.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Enhanced Tracking Protection.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Prevent cross-site tracking / Block all cookies.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions → Manage and delete cookies.

You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the official Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.
