Sunnahs of Friday

  • Ghusl (غسل): Performing the ritual bath before heading to the prayer.
  • Clean Clothes: Wearing your best and cleanest clothes.
  • Perfume: Applying perfume (for men).
  • Siwak (سواك): Using the miswak to clean your teeth.
  • Surah Al-Kahf (سورة الكهف): Reciting Surah Al-Kahf during the day or the night before.
  • Prayer on the Prophet (الصلاة على النبي ﷺ): Sending abundant blessings upon the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH).
  • Dua (الدعاء): Making earnest supplications, especially during the 'hour of acceptance'.
