Become a Member of the Muslim Students Association at Politecnico di Milano (ASM)!

🏷️ Membership Fee: €5

ASM is a community-centered association for Muslim students at Politecnico di Milano. Our goal is to create a warm, cultural, and supportive environment, helping international students feel at home, even when they’re far from home.

🌟 Why Join Us?

  • • Exclusive Discounts: on events, such as Islamic conferences, cultural excursions, sports activities, and more.
  • • Priority Reservations: for you and your friends at popular events, so you never miss a spot.
  • • Continuous Support Network: that offers a sense of community and helps you adjust to life abroad.

🌱 Your Impact

With your membership, we can continue to organize meaningful activities that enrich our campus community, including:

  • • Ramadan Iftars
  • • Mentorship Programs
  • • Workshops and Guest Lectures

Your €5 membership fee helps us keep these events accessible and affordable for all.

Special Welcome Gift 🎁 Free Personalized 3D Keychain!

Every new registered member receives a custom 3D-printed keychain with their personalized name as a welcome gift from ASM. You can choose the name you want printed on your keychain in the next steps!

🤝 ASM Teams

ASM provides the opportunity to join its teams, practice your skills and contribute to its ongoing story of success. A brief description of the teams and their role is provided below:

• Graphics and Video making: involved in designing posters for events and editing photos and videos.
• Marketing and Social Media: manages the social media pages of the association, promotes events, and keeps members updated.
• Events and Trips: proposes and organizes activities and trips of ASM. For each activity there is a contact person aided by team mates.
• Tutoring: elder students help first-year students. (Italian language is mandatory)
• Jumuah: responsible for weekly Khatib scheduling in both campuses, Drive revision of texts, and maintaining the meditation room.
